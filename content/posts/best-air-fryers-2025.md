Air fryers have gone from a niche gadget to a kitchen essential in just a few years — and for good reason. They crisp up fries, roast vegetables, and even bake small batches of cookies using a fraction of the oil (and time) of a conventional oven. But with dozens of models on the market, picking the right one can be overwhelming.

In 2025, the air fryer landscape splits into three main categories: classic **basket-style** air fryers (compact, fast, and affordable), **oven-style** air fryers (larger capacity, more versatile), and **dual-zone** models (cook two foods at once, each with their own settings). We tested and compared the top contenders across all three categories to help you find the right one for your cooking style, household size, and budget — from under $50 to around $200.

## What to Look For in an Air Fryer

Before diving into our top picks, here are the key factors to consider when shopping for an air fryer in 2025:

### Capacity

Air fryer capacity is measured in quarts, and it directly determines how much food you can cook in one batch. **2–4 quarts** is ideal for 1–2 people (a couple of chicken breasts or a small side of fries). **5–6 quarts** handles a meal for 3–4 people (a whole pound of wings or a small chicken). **8+ quarts** and dual-zone models can cook a full family meal — main and side — simultaneously. If you're regularly cooking for more than two people, don't go below 5 quarts.

### Wattage and Heating Technology

Wattage ranges from about 1200W in compact models to 1800W in larger oven-style units. Higher wattage means faster preheating and crispier results — but it's not everything. Look for models with a convection fan that distributes heat evenly; some premium units now feature dual-speed fans or top-and-bottom heating elements for more even browning without shaking or flipping.

### Preset Programs

Most air fryers include one-touch presets for common foods like fries, chicken, steak, fish, and veggies. Entry-level models offer 4–7 presets, while feature-packed units go up to 10–12. Presets aren't essential — you can always set time and temperature manually — but they're genuinely convenient for everyday cooking and reduce the guesswork for beginners.

### Ease of Cleaning

The non-stick coating on the basket or tray is the single biggest factor in how much you'll actually enjoy using your air fryer. All the models we recommend have dishwasher-safe, non-stick baskets or crisper plates. Oven-style models with a pull-out crumb tray are easier to keep clean than those without. If you hate scrubbing, prioritize models with ceramic or high-quality non-stick coatings.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a basket air fryer and an oven-style air fryer?

**Basket air fryers** use a pull-out drawer with a perforated basket — they preheat faster, cook food more quickly, and crisp more aggressively thanks to the compact cooking chamber and direct airflow. **Oven-style air fryers** (also called air fryer toaster ovens) have more capacity and are more versatile — you can fit a whole chicken or a 12-inch pizza — but they preheat slower and take up more counter space. Choose basket if speed and crispness are your priority; choose oven if you want to replace multiple appliances.

### How many quarts do I need for my household?

For **1–2 people**, a 4-quart basket air fryer is plenty. For **3–4 people**, go for 5–6 quarts. For **4+ people** or if you regularly cook main and side dishes at the same time, a dual-zone model like the Ninja DZ201 (8 quarts total) or an oven-style model like the Cuisinart TOA-60 is the best choice.

### Are air fryers actually healthier than deep frying?

Yes — air fryers use convection heat and a small amount of oil (typically 1–2 teaspoons vs. cups in deep frying). Studies show air-fried foods can contain 70–80% less fat than their deep-fried counterparts. That said, an air fryer isn't a magic health machine — the nutritional quality still depends on what you put in it.

### Can I put aluminum foil or parchment paper in an air fryer?

Yes, but with caution. Parchment paper is fine as long as it's weighed down by food (loose paper can fly up into the heating element). Aluminum foil is safe in most models but can block airflow in basket-style fryers — only use it to line the bottom tray in oven-style models. Many brands now sell pre-cut parchment liners sized specifically for their baskets.

### How much electricity does an air fryer use?

Most air fryers draw 1500–1800 watts while actively cooking. For a typical 15–25 minute cooking session, that's about 0.4–0.75 kWh — roughly 5–10 cents of electricity in most US regions. Compared to preheating a full-size oven (2–3 kWh per session), air fryers are significantly more energy-efficient for small to medium meals.
